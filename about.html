About Sand Seed Academy

Sand Seed Academy is a daycare and pre-school founded in late 2025 by Motswana entrepreneur Rapelang Monnaatlala. Built with one mission in mind — to provide affordable, high-quality early childhood education to every family in Ghanzi and its surrounding areas.

Our school was born out of a deep love for community and a belief that every child, regardless of their background, deserves the best possible start in life. We are proud to create local job opportunities while building an institute where children can learn, play, and truly flourish.

At Sand Seed Academy, our curriculum goes far beyond textbooks. We offer structured indoor lessons, creative outdoor activities, and nutritious daily meals — because we do not just train young minds, we take care of the whole child. Our professionally trained staff bring warmth, patience, and passion to everything they do, ensuring that every learner feels safe, seen, and supported.
