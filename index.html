Welcome to Sand Seed Academy

At Sand Seed Academy, we believe that every child deserves a strong and loving start to their education. Located in the heart of Ghanzi, Botswana, we offer affordable, high-quality early childhood education to families in our community and surrounding areas.

Our extraordinary staff are professionally trained to teach and care for your little ones. We do not just train their minds — we nurture their bodies, creativity, and confidence every single day.

Did You Know?

Sand Seed Academy opened its doors in late 2025 and has already welcomed over 80 students into our growing family. We are proud to be Ghanzi's newest and most affordable pre-school.

Services Offered at Sand Seed Academy

Indoor Learning

Our classrooms are bright, welcoming spaces where learning comes alive through visuals, storytelling, and interactive lessons carefully designed for young minds.

Student Meals

Every child learns better on a full stomach. We provide freshly prepared, nutritious meals to fuel our students' growing minds and bodies throughout the day.

Outdoor Learning

Beyond the classroom, our hands-on outdoor sessions build creativity, teamwork, and fine motor skills — because learning is most powerful when children can touch, move, and explore.
